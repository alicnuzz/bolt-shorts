# Privacy Policy — Bolt Knows Why

Bolt Knows Why is a small automation that uploads our own short videos to our own YouTube channel (@BoltKnowsWhy).

- The app only uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and read basic statistics of our own channel.
- It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data of viewers or other users.
- OAuth tokens are stored privately as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets and are used only by the channel owner.
- You can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: via the YouTube channel @BoltKnowsWhy.
